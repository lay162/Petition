Ofqual's Own Lawyers: "We Don't Do SEN!"

Their own legal team confirmed: "Ofqual has no duties under Part 3 of the Children and Families Act 2014" and "the SEND Code of Practice does not apply to Ofqual".

Despite ministerial claims that "Ofqual can oversee SEN qualifications," their solicitors have now legally confirmed: the Regulated Qualifications Framework was never designed for SEN/EHCP learners.

Every day without LUNA SEN Regulator, children with autism, ADHD, dyslexia, and other needs are forced into a system that admits it wasn't designed for them. This isn't about "reasonable adjustments" - it's about fundamental design failure that's destroying futures daily.

Your signature tells ministers:
"Stop failing our children who have SEN/EHCP."

"Recognise, authorise and implement"
LUNA SEN Regulator - we are the solution England desperately needs."

See the evidence Sign the petition
"The RQF was not designed or structured for learners with special educational needs and disabilities (SEND) or SEN/EHCP learners with Education, Health and Care Plans (EHCPs)."

— Ofqual's Legal Team (Gowling WLG), Official Response, 19 Sept 2025

We demand government recognition, authorisation, and implementation of the LUNA SEN Regulator as England's official SEN regulator - working parallel to Ofqual with the LSRQF (LUNA SEN Regulated Qualifications Framework) designed specifically for SEN/EHCP learners.

The law engaged

  • Children & Families Act 2014 Part 3: SEN/EHCP legal duties unfulfilled for 10+ years since RQF implementation (2015).
  • Equality Act 2010: Section 19 (sustained indirect discrimination), Section 149 (ongoing Public Sector Equality Duty breach).
  • SEND Code of Practice: Statutory guidance not enforced for SEN learners for over a decade.
  • Human Rights Act 1998: Protocol 1 Article 2 (ongoing breach of right to education), Article 14 (sustained discrimination).
  • UNCRPD Article 24: Persistent failure to provide inclusive education system for 10+ years.

The remedy

  • Recognise the LUNA SEN Regulator as England's official SEN regulator.
  • Authorise the LUNA SEN Regulator to operate parallel to Ofqual.
  • Implement the LUNA SEN Regulated Qualifications Framework (LSRQF) designed specifically for SEN/EHCP learners.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the LUNA SEN Regulator? +
It's a proposal to close the regulation gap for SEND learners in the UK so neurodiverse children are recognised, protected, and given equal opportunities.
Who can sign this petition? +
Anyone who supports fair protection and recognition for SEND learners across the UK - parents, teachers, employers, and community members.
How will my details be used? +
Only to record your support and, if you consent, to send campaign updates. See our Privacy Notice for full details.
What is the LSRQF? +
The Specialist SEN Qualifications Framework - a dedicated regulatory system designed specifically for neurodiverse learners, unlike the current RQF which wasn't built for SEN needs.
Why is this important now? +
Ofqual's own solicitors confirmed the RQF wasn't designed for SEN learners. This creates a fundamental gap that's destroying futures daily. We need proper recognition now.
What happens after I sign? +
Your signature helps build momentum for the campaign. We'll keep you updated on progress and may contact you about further actions to support the LUNA SEN Regulator.
