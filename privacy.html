Privacy Policy

Data Controller

Lauren Moore, LUNA SEN Regulator

Contact: petition@lunasenregulator.org.uk

Lawful Basis for Processing

We process your personal data under UK GDPR Article 6(1)(a) — Consent. You provide explicit consent when you sign up for campaign updates.

Data We Collect

If you choose to join our campaign updates, we collect:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Postcode (for constituency mapping)
  • Role (Parent, Young Person, SENCo, Teacher, LA, Employer, Other)
  • Consent confirmation

Purpose of Processing

We use your data solely to:

  • Send you updates about the SRQF petition campaign
  • Present constituency-level evidence to MPs
  • Keep you informed about related policy developments

We never sell or share your data with third parties.

Data Retention

We retain your data for a maximum of 24 months after the petition closes, or sooner if you request deletion.

Your Rights

Under UK GDPR, you have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Rectify inaccurate data
  • Erase your data ("right to be forgotten")
  • Restrict processing
  • Object to processing
  • Data portability
  • Withdraw consent at any time

Unsubscribe & Contact

To unsubscribe, request data deletion, or exercise your rights:

Email: petition@lunasenregulator.org.uk

We will respond within 30 days.

Parliament Petition

Important: The official Parliament petition is managed separately by the UK Parliament and has its own privacy policy. Signing the Parliament petition is the only signature counted by ministers.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
