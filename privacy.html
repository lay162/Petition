Privacy Policy

Data Controller

Lauren Moore, LUNA SEN Regulator

Contact: petition@lunasenregulator.org.uk

Lawful Basis for Processing

We process your personal data under UK GDPR Article 6(1)(a) — Consent. You provide explicit consent when you sign up for campaign updates.

Data We Collect

If you choose to join our campaign updates, we collect:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Postcode (for constituency mapping)
  • Role (Parent, Young Person, SENCo, Teacher, LA, Employer, Other)
  • Optional testimonial/voice submission (if provided)
  • Consent confirmation

Purpose of Processing

We use your data solely to:

  • Send you updates about the LSRQF petition campaign
  • Present constituency-level evidence to MPs
  • Keep you informed about related policy developments
  • Display optional testimonials on our website (if you provide them and consent)

We never sell your data. Your data is processed using Google Workspace (Sheets) as a data processor for storage and email functionality only. Google is contractually obligated to protect your data and cannot use it for their own purposes.

Data Retention

We retain your data for a maximum of 24 months after the petition closes, or sooner if you request deletion.

Your Rights

Under UK GDPR, you have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Rectify inaccurate data
  • Erase your data ("right to be forgotten")
  • Restrict processing
  • Object to processing
  • Data portability
  • Withdraw consent at any time

Unsubscribe & Contact

To unsubscribe, request data deletion, or exercise your rights:

Email: petition@lunasenregulator.org.uk

We will respond within 30 days.

Data Storage & Transfers

Your data is stored in Google Sheets (Google Workspace) for the purpose of managing campaign updates and constituency mapping. Google is based in the United States and may process your data outside the UK/EEA.

We have ensured appropriate safeguards are in place through Google's Standard Contractual Clauses and Google's commitment to data protection standards. Your data is only accessed by authorised personnel at LUNA SEN Regulator.

Cookies

We use only strictly necessary cookies to enable form submission functionality. These cookies are essential for the website to function and cannot be disabled.

We do not use analytics, advertising, or tracking cookies. No personal data is collected via cookies beyond what is necessary for form processing.

If you submit a form, Google Apps Script may set temporary session cookies required for processing your submission. These cookies are deleted when your session ends.

Complaints

If you have concerns about how we handle your personal data, you have the right to lodge a complaint with the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO), the UK's independent data protection authority:

  • ICO Website: ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint
  • ICO Helpline: 0303 123 1113

We encourage you to contact us first at petition@lunasenregulator.org.uk to resolve any concerns.

Parliament Petition

Important: The official Parliament petition is managed separately by the UK Parliament and has its own privacy policy. Signing the Parliament petition is the only signature counted by ministers.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
